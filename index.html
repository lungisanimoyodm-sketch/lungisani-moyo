A good website doesn't matter if no one finds it

Most of the businesses I work with have a website already. The issue usually isn't the site — it's what happens before someone arrives at it.

Your Google Business Profile is incomplete

Missing categories, no recent posts, and unanswered reviews all quietly reduce how often you appear in the local "map pack."

Your site ranks for the wrong searches

Pages get built for what the business wants to say, not for the words customers actually type into Google.

There's no way to see what's working

Without Search Console or GBP insights connected and reviewed, it's guesswork whether last month's changes helped at all.

Technical issues sit unnoticed

Slow pages, broken redirects, and thin metadata cap how well even good content can perform.
